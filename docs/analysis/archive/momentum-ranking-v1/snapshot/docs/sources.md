# Research Source Leads

These are external research/source leads used during the chat. They are **not** a substitute for project evidence and should be re-verified before implementation-critical claims or thresholds are adopted.

## Market microstructure / order flow

- Cont, Kukanov, Stoikov — *The Price Impact of Order Book Events*  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402  
  Research lead: short-horizon price change and order-flow imbalance at best quotes.

- Gould / Bonart — queue imbalance and next mid-price direction  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03492  
  Research lead: best-queue imbalance predictive relation; stronger in some tick regimes.

- Multi-level OFI research lead  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.13213  
  Research lead: possible incremental information from deeper book levels.

## TASE-specific

- Bank of Israel — algorithmic traders on the Tel Aviv Stock Exchange  
  https://www.boi.org.il/en/economic-roles/research-and-publications/all-research/discussion-paper-series-research-department/the-strategies-employed-by-algorithmic-traders-on-the-tel-aviv-stock-exchange-and-the-connection-between-them-and-indicators-of-trading-quality/  
  Research lead: quote-generation activity, executions, market-making/liquidity behavior on TASE.

## Trade classification / transaction direction

- Lee–Ready style trade classification literature was used as conceptual background: trade price relative to prevailing midpoint can be evidence about aggressor side, but classification is imperfect and requires synchronized trade/quote data.

## Execution / first passage / calibration

The chat also used literature leads on:
- first-passage/time-to-target framing;
- execution shortfall, limit-order fill uncertainty and queue position;
- microstructure noise and bid-ask bounce;
- feature redundancy / ablation / calibration.

Exact citations for these should be re-collected into issue-specific research artifacts when those topics become active, rather than relying on this abbreviated index.

## Rule

External literature informs hypotheses. TASE/project data decides whether the signal is useful here.
