# D-023 — Progressive context loading with preserved cold history

Status: Accepted

Decision:

Market Flow uses a three-tier AI context model:

~~~text
HOT
→ mandatory compact fresh-chat context

WARM
→ task-specific code/spec/test/policy context

COLD
→ preserved history, detailed rationale and completed-work evidence
~~~

Fresh chats must be self-sufficient from GitHub, but they must not preload all historical evidence.

Historical knowledge is preserved under explicit history/index locations and loaded when a concrete trigger requires it.

STATUS.json remains a compact live operational pointer rather than an accumulated development diary.

Why:

- new chats cannot rely on conversation history;
- loading all repository history on every fresh chat wastes tokens and increases contradiction risk;
- deleting history makes future diagnosis and rationale recovery harder;
- progressive disclosure gives both correctness and efficiency.
